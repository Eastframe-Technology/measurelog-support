Privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026. MeasureLog is published by Eastframe Technology LLC.

What we collect: nothing. MeasureLog has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting service. It sends no data to us or anyone else, and the app itself has no internet access.

Camera. The camera is used only while you measure. Nothing is recorded except the photo you choose to save with a measurement.

Your measurements and photos are stored only on your device, until you delete them or uninstall the app. If you share one, it goes to the app or service you pick in the share sheet.

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We do not see your payment details.

Google Play Services for AR. On Android, this app runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children. MeasureLog is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes. A new version of this policy will be published at this address with a new effective date.
