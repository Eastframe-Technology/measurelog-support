The formula

An inch is exactly 2.54 cm, by international definition. A foot is 12 inches, so exactly 30.48 cm.

cm = (feet × 12 + inches) × 2.54

total inches = cm / 2.54, then feet = whole part of (total inches / 12), and the rest is inches.

For a fraction, the page rounds the total inches to the nearest mark and then reduces it, so 8/16 shows as 1/2. If the rounding reaches a whole inch or a whole foot, it carries over: 11.99 inches shows as 1 ft 0 in, not 11 16/16 in.

Worked example

Convert 5 ft 10 1/2 in to metric. That is 5 × 12 + 10.5 = 70.5 inches. Then 70.5 × 2.54 = 179.07 cm, or 1.7907 m.

Going back, start from 180 cm. 180 / 2.54 = 70.866 inches. 70 inches is 5 ft 10 in, leaving 0.866 in. To the nearest sixteenth, 0.866 × 16 = 13.9, which rounds to 14/16, or 7/8. The result is 5 ft 10 7/8 in, about 0.2 mm off the exact value.

Limits

  • A conversion can't add precision. If you measured to the nearest centimeter, two decimals of an inch mean nothing.
  • Rounding adds up. Add measurements up first, then convert. Converting and rounding each one piles up small errors.
  • Survey feet are not covered. This uses the international foot of 0.3048 m, the one in ordinary use. The difference is tiny for anything in a building.

Frequently asked

How many cm is 5 ft 10 in? 5 ft 10 in is 70 inches. 70 × 2.54 = 177.8 cm, which is 1.778 m.

How do I convert cm to feet and inches? Divide the cm by 2.54 to get inches. Divide those by 12: the whole number is the feet, and the remainder is the inches. So 180 cm is 70.87 in, which is 5 ft 10.87 in, or 5 ft 10 7/8 in to the nearest eighth.

Why is the fraction not exact? Tape measures mark halves, quarters, eighths and sixteenths, and most metric values fall between those marks. The page tells you how far the fraction is from the exact value, in millimeters.

Can I type a fraction? Yes. Type 10 1/2 or 10.5 in the inches box, or use the fraction menu. A comma works as a decimal point.

Does this page send my numbers anywhere? No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing you type leaves it.
