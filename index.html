What the app does

Point, tap, measure

A dot on screen snaps to surfaces the phone has found. Tap + to drop a point. The length updates live as you move to the next one, and points chain into several lengths in a row.

Saved with a photo

Each measurement keeps a photo with the lines and lengths drawn on it, with a title, so you can search for it later. Rename, delete or share.

Closed shapes (Pro)

Close a shape to get its perimeter and area, handy for a rug, a table top or a floor.

A calibrated ruler

An on-screen ruler for small objects. Calibrate it once with a bank card, which is 85.60 mm wide. It needs no AR.

Your units

cm, m, inches, feet and inches. Switch at any time, including on saved measurements.

Honest limits

No stud finder and no fake features. A camera can't see inside a wall. How accurate phone AR measuring really is.

Where it helps

  • Buying furniture. Will the sofa fit between the door and the radiator? A rough answer with a photo beats guessing in the shop.
  • Planning a room. Get a first idea of floor area before you measure properly with a tape.
  • Keeping sizes. The bathroom window, the alcove, the car trunk: saved, with a picture, for the day you need them.
  • Small things. A screw, a card or a plug, against the calibrated ruler.

For anything you will cut or order to size, check with a real tape.

Frequently asked

Can I download it now? Not yet. MeasureLog is not in any store at the moment. An Android version using ARCore is being built, and we have no release date to share. The tools on this site work today in any browser.

How accurate is it? On iPhones and iPads with LiDAR (Pro models), usually within 1%. Without LiDAR, expect a few centimeters over a couple of meters. Android phones track with ARCore: expect a few centimeters of error over a couple of meters, sometimes more on plain surfaces. Move slowly, measure from a steady position, and check anything critical with a real tape measure. More on accuracy.

It says to move my phone. Why? Augmented reality needs to see the room to find surfaces. Move slowly, point at a textured, well-lit surface, and avoid plain white walls in dim light.

Is there a stud finder? No. A phone camera can't see inside walls, so MeasureLog doesn't pretend to.

How do I calibrate the ruler? In the Ruler tab, tap Calibrate, put any bank card on the screen and adjust until the frame matches its width.

What does the free version include? AR measuring, the ruler and 5 saved measurements. MeasureLog Pro, a single purchase, adds unlimited saves with notes, closed shapes with perimeter and area, and sharing.

Is there a subscription? No. MeasureLog Pro is a single purchase. Restore it on a new device from Settings.
