MeasureLog

An AR tape measure that remembers: lengths, perimeter and area with your camera, saved with a photo. Free, with one optional unlock. No ads, no subscription, no account.

Questions, bugs, or feature requests: email contact@eastframe-technology.com. A human reads every message, usually the same day.

Frequently asked

How accurate is it? On iPhones and iPads with LiDAR (Pro models), usually within 1%. Without LiDAR, expect a few centimeters over a couple of meters. Move slowly, measure from a steady position, and check anything critical with a real tape measure.

It says to move my phone. Why? Augmented reality needs to see the room to find surfaces. Move slowly, point at a textured, well-lit surface, and avoid plain white walls in dim light.

Is there a stud finder? No. A phone camera can't see inside walls, so MeasureLog doesn't pretend to.

How do I calibrate the ruler? In the Ruler tab, tap Calibrate, put any bank card on the screen and adjust until the frame matches its width.

What does the free version include? AR measuring, the ruler and 5 saved measurements. MeasureLog Pro, a single purchase, adds unlimited saves with notes, closed shapes with perimeter and area, and sharing.

Is there a subscription? No. MeasureLog Pro is a single purchase. Restore it on a new device from Settings.

Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026. MeasureLog is published by Eastframe Technology LLC.

What we collect: nothing. MeasureLog has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting service. It never connects to the internet and sends no data to us or anyone else.

Camera. The camera is used only while you measure. Nothing is recorded except the photo you choose to save with a measurement.

Your measurements and photos are stored only on your device, until you delete them or uninstall the app. If you share one, it goes to the app or service you pick in the share sheet.

Purchases are handled by the App Store. We do not see your payment details.

Children. MeasureLog is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
